Month to month is where the cost hides

Financing has never been easier to say yes to. Pay in four at checkout. A point-of-sale loan for the new couch. A home improvement plan from the contractor. A card for everything else. Each one is presented as a payment you can handle this month.

None of them show you the total. Not the interest, not the fees, not how many years you will be paying, and not what happens to your checking account when all of those payments land in the same week.

Roughly 6 in 10 Americans live paycheck to paycheck. When every decision is made one month at a time, the full cost of borrowing stays invisible until it becomes a problem.

The full picture, in four questions

TYDES is built to answer the questions a monthly payment never does.

1

What do I owe?

Every balance in one place: cards, buy now pay later plans, personal and point-of-sale loans, home improvement financing, and anything you add manually. Balances, minimums, and due dates side by side instead of scattered across a dozen apps and statements.

2

What is it really costing me?

Interest at each account’s actual rate, plus the fees that pile on when a payment slips. “Just $90 a month” becomes a real dollar figure you can see, compare, and decide on.

3

How long will I be paying?

A payoff horizon for every balance at the payment you are making now, so a “manageable” monthly payment shows its true length in months and years.

4

Can I actually cover it?

Cash Flow starts from your real checking balance, lines up what is due and when, and shows what is left. You see the squeeze before it happens, not after the overdraft.

How it works

  1. Connect your accounts through Plaid, or add accounts and payment plans manually. TYDES asks for clear consent before anything is connected.
  2. TYDES pulls balances, statement amounts, minimums, rates, and due dates for your liabilities, and a starting balance from the checking account you choose, without displaying transaction line items.
  3. Add the recurring events that matter, like paychecks and rent, then see the whole picture across the Dashboard, Accounts, Schedule, and Cash Flow views.
  4. Get a payment-review notification before a payment is due, so nothing slips into late fees.

Security and Data Handling

  • TYDES uses encrypted connections to protect data in transit.
  • Sign in with Apple is used for account authentication.
  • Sensitive connected-account data is protected with encryption at rest.
  • TYDES uses authorized financial data only to support bill tracking, liability insights, and Cash Flow planning.
  • TYDES does not sell personal information or use your financial data for third-party advertising.
